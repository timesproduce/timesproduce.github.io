# Our clients are at the heart of everything we do.

With over 70 years in the produce industry and roots in California farming communities, Times Produce is a Los Angeles-based wholesale distributor serving customers worldwide.

## What we focus on

<div class="pillars">
<div>

### Customization
We offer flexible solutions. Wholesale clients can choose straight or mixed loads, and specialized clients can consolidate fresh, frozen, or dry produce.

</div>
<div>

### Global Reach
We work with small and large farmers, brokers, and distributors worldwide, offering a range of quality and price options and items not usually found on the open market.

</div>
<div>

### Quality
Our facility is covered by the PrimusGFS food safety program, a leader in food safety worldwide.
