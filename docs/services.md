# Quality, integrity & transparency

Times Produce started at Ninth Street City Market and has over 70 years in the Los Angeles produce market. We built relationships with local and international farmers, and in 2021 moved to our Bay Street facility, where we modernized our business model and operating systems.

## Hospitality
We serve restaurants, hotels, schools, casinos, and other institutions with fresh produce, specialty and organic items, and staff training to help manage profitability.

## Retail
We offer produce department layouts, merchandising concepts, and promotional support.

## Import/Export
We source fruits and vegetables and ship them worldwide, with an emphasis on cost efficiency, consistency, and reliability.
