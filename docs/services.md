<h1>Honest produce, done right</h1>
<p>We started out at the Ninth Street City Market and have been part of the Los Angeles produce scene for over 70 years. Our long friendships with farmers, here and abroad, are a big reason we've done well.</p>

<h2 class="sm">Hospitality</h2>
<p>Restaurants, hotels, schools, casinos and other big kitchens count on us for fresh fruits and vegetables, specialty items and organic options. Our team can also train your staff to help you waste less and make more.</p>
<h2 class="sm">Retail</h2>
<p>We know the retail side well. If you're setting up a produce department, we can help with layouts, display ideas and promotions.</p>
<h2 class="sm">Import/Export</h2>
<p>We find the fruits and vegetables you're looking for and ship them anywhere in the world. We keep costs down and make sure you get the same good quality every time.</p>
